# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to build something and is short on
time. Critique the code, not the coder.

## Our standards

Things that make this project work:

- Technical arguments backed by evidence — a repro, a benchmark, a spec link, a failing test.
- Saying "I was wrong" quickly when you are.
- Reviewing a diff on its merits, independent of who wrote it.
- Remembering that most contributors here are not native English speakers. Issues and pull
  requests in **English or Spanish** are equally welcome.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or a group.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time: spam, low-effort AI-generated pull requests that
  were never run, or reopening a settled decision without new information.

## Security is not a conduct matter

If you found a vulnerability — including a way to bypass the `validate_message` policy
wedge — do **not** open an issue. Report it privately: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to **contacto@wizneo.org**, or through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/WIZNEOAI/Manychat-MCP/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather not email. Either reaches us without a public thread.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete contributions and comments, and may block accounts.
We will explain a moderation decision if you ask, but this is a small project and we will not
debate it at length.

## Scope

Applies in this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
wherever someone is representing the project in public.
